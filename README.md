# 365 Days of Cyber 🛡️

Welcome to my 365-day cybersecurity journey! 

I am a third-year ICT student at Mzuzu University documenting my daily progress as I transition from a beginner to a professional in Cybersecurity and Penetration Testing. 

This repository serves as my public lab notebook. Here, I upload my daily notes, lab walkthroughs, scripts, and proof of work.

---

## 🎯 The Mission
To spend 365 days learning, breaking, and securing systems. I believe in **Learning in Public**—sharing the wins, the failures, and the troubleshooting process. 

*   **Start Date:** September 22, 2026
*   **Current Status:** In Progress (Day 60+)
*   **Primary Platforms:** TryHackMe, DVWA, Metasploitable

---

## 🗂️ Repository Structure

*   `📁 01-Notes/` - Daily markdown logs of what I learned, commands used, and key takeaways.
*   `📁 02-Scripts/` - Bash and Python scripts I've written for automation and network monitoring.
*   `📁 03-Labs/` - Detailed walkthroughs and write-ups for CTFs (TryHackMe, etc.) and vulnerable VMs (DVWA).
*   `📁 04-Reports/` - Practice penetration testing reports (Executive Summaries, Findings, Remediation).
